How the calculation works

The weekday or weekend time period generating the highest demand indicates the shared-parking requirement for this combination of uses, as described in the workbook. Reduction = total required parking − highest combined demand.

Results remain decimal values; no whole-space rounding is applied. Enter up to two decimal places. Blank rows count as zero. Inputs stay on this page and reset when you reload or leave it.
